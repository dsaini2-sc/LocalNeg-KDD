\subsection{Evaluation panel}
The panel's queries are the users with at least one accepted friend request in the three days after the training window, and their
positives are those requests. Candidates are a fixed uniform hash sample of 50M users together with every positive target, 85.3M users in
all. Ranking is exact over the candidates. Macro recall@$k$ is the per-query share of positives ranked in the top $k$, averaged over
queries.

\subsection{Training cost}

planner exposes under 60\,s over the whole run and runs its clustering (1{,}600--1{,}760\,s in total) on a CPU thread while the GPU trains.
Full-state checkpoints of the cached arms are larger because the 2.7\,GB per-worker cache is part of the resumable state.

\subsection{Cap feasibility}
A window whose largest cluster holds more rows than the cap can place across the window's batches cannot be scheduled exactly. The
scheduler used in every capped run of this paper stops with an error on such a window instead of relaxing the cap. The one exception is
a final window shorter than the refresh interval, which is scheduled with an exact equal-batch order that still respects the cap. Every
capped run reported here completed, so every window was scheduled under the strict cap.
