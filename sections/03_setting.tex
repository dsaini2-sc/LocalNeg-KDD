\section{Setting}
\label{sec:setting}

\head{Graph and task} Friend recommendation is framed as retrieval over a user graph of 966M nodes with three
relations: friendship (67.9B directed edges), bidirectional engagement (15.0B) and mutual contact-book membership
(21.3B), 104.2B in total. The graph is a snapshot taken before the label window, so no training edge is created by a
label. A positive is a friend request sent in the 14 days after the snapshot and accepted within two days. Data
preparation keeps at most four positives per source. This leaves 211M distinct positive pairs over 104M sources, and
169M positive rows eligible for training.

\head{Model and training} A graph-transformer encoder embeds each user from its sampled neighbourhood into 128
dimensions. The fanouts are 10 friends, 20 then 10 engagement neighbours, and 10 contact-book neighbours. A two-layer
decoder maps the anchor's embedding into a query space, and candidates are scored against it by their encoder
embeddings. A training example is a positive row, an anchor paired with one of its positives. The loss is InfoNCE over
the positives of the other rows in the batch and 8{,}192 shared random negatives per step. Accidental hits are removed,
and a logQ frequency correction is estimated with count-min sketches~\cite{yi2019sampling}. Each of 16 A100 workers
trains on 2{,}048 rows per step. Every run starts from the same 200-update random-batch prefix and then trains
15{,}479 further updates, three passes over the eligible rows.

\head{Evaluation} We evaluate on a frozen panel built from the three days of labels after the training window: 38.96M
queries with 156.8M positives. They are ranked against 85.3M candidates, a fixed uniform sample of 50M users together
with every positive target. Search is exact within the panel. The headline metric is macro recall@1000, the per-query
mean, because the retrieval stage hands up to 1{,}000 candidates to ranking. We also report recall at 10, 100 and 500 and
MRR. Every number is from the final model of a run; no checkpoint is selected.
