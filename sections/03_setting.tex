\section{Setting}
\label{sec:setting}

\subsection{Graph and task}

Friend recommendation is framed as retrieval over a user graph of 966M nodes with three relations: friendship
(67.9B directed edges), bidirectional engagement (15.0B) and mutual contact-book membership (21.3B), 104.2B in total. The graph is a snapshot taken before the label window, so no training edge is created by a label. A positive is a friend
request sent in the 14 days after the snapshot and accepted within two days. Data preparation keeps at most four
positives per source, which leaves 211M distinct positive pairs over 104M sources, and 169M positive rows eligible for
training.

\subsection{Model and training}

A graph-transformer encoder embeds each user from its sampled neighbourhood (fanouts of 10 friends, 20 then 10 engagement
neighbours, and 10 contact-book neighbours) into 128 dimensions. A two-layer decoder maps the anchor's embedding into a
query space; candidates are scored against it by their encoder embeddings. A training example is a positive row, an
anchor paired with one of its positives. The loss is InfoNCE over the positives of the other rows in the batch and 8{,}192
shared random negatives per step, with accidental hits removed and a logQ frequency correction estimated with
count-min sketches~\cite{yi2019sampling}. Each of 16 A100 workers trains on 2{,}048 rows per step. Every run starts from the
same 200-update random-batch prefix and then trains 15{,}479 further updates, three passes over the eligible rows.

\subsection{Evaluation}

We evaluate on a frozen panel built from the three days of labels after the training window: 38.96M queries with
156.8M positives, ranked against 85.3M candidates, a fixed uniform sample of 50M users together with every positive
target. Search is exact within the panel. The headline metric is macro recall@1000, the per-query mean, because the
retrieval stage hands 1{,}000 candidates to ranking; we also report recall at 10, 100 and 500 and MRR. Every number is
from the final model of a run; no checkpoint is selected.
